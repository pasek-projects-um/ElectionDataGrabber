# Registry

The registry layer separates identity, denominator accounting, source discovery, source capability, and execution evidence. Do not use a single CSV as a proxy for “coverage.”

## First-pass state result surfaces

`first_pass_state_result_surfaces.csv` is the breadth-first 51-jurisdiction catalog: all 50 states plus DC have at least one plausible official result surface recorded.

Presence means **discovered/catalogued**. It does not mean the source is fetchable today, parser-supported, normalized, replay-tested, refresh-verified, or complete within the state.

## Within-state geography and authority coverage

Primary locality denominators and canonical locality records answer a different question: which election geographies/authorities are expected and individually enumerated? Geography/source ledgers preserve all credible source leads per unit and keep unresolved units visible.

One source may cover many jurisdictions; one jurisdiction may have multiple sources. Source count is never a denominator.

## Capability versus execution

Positive final/election-night capability requires evidence-bearing adjudication. Candidate URLs, platform fingerprints, external research leads, and failed/successful fetch probes do not automatically create capability.

Execution maturity is tracked separately:

`discovered → fetchable → parser_selected → parsed → normalized → replay_tested`

Election-night refresh verification is a further operational property and should not be inferred from a one-time successful parse.

## External lead corpora

External research/scraper repositories are retained with provenance because they are valuable for endpoint discovery, historical URL reconstruction, platform-family identification, and QA. They are not authoritative result feeds unless the underlying official artifact/source is identified and preserved.

## Generated audits

Files under `audit/` may be generated by CI/scripts to expose gaps, readiness, probes, and execution evidence. Treat checked-in registries as durable inputs/contracts and generated audits as reproducible views unless a specific file documents otherwise.
